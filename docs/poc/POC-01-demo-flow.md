# POC-01 — deterministic demonstration flow

**Status: DEMO / À VALIDER MAS.** Scope: [#7](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/7), [#36](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/36). This storyboard uses the three service IDs and provenance in [the service profiles](POC-01-service-profiles.md). It specifies future mock behavior; it does not implement the assistant, uploads, OCR, appointments, or worker screen. The run is offline, repeatable, accessible without an official account, and uses synthetic data only. Every screen shows “Simulation — aucune demande déposée auprès de l'administration.”

## Locked golden path A: 60–120 seconds

The fixed story is Fatma, a fictional unemployed mother of children aged 4 and 9. The service is `AMEN_SOCIAL_REGISTRATION`; the aim is **préparation de dossier / état de complétude du scénario de démonstration**, not a determination of entitlement. The official Amen Social pages describe registration pieces and territorial ULPS review; the demo's four sample pieces are non-exhaustive. [A1](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/node/1843), [A2](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/node/304), [A3](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/demande-dinscription-au-programme-amen-social)

**Scripted input and question:** Fatma types “نحب نحضّر ملف الأمان الاجتماعي. ما نخدمش وعندي صغيرين.” or “nheb n7adher dossier Amen Social, ma nekhdemch w 3andi zouz sghar.” The deterministic router returns `prepare_amen_registration`, then asks one question: “Souhaitez-vous préparer un dossier de démonstration pour votre foyer ?” She answers yes. The wording in Derja and Arabizi is provisional and needs native-speaker review; no model infers legal eligibility from unemployment or family size.

## Lightweight route B: children's family allowances

**Intent:** “Je veux connaître les aides pour mes enfants.” / “نحب نعرف شنية المنح اللي تنجم تعاون صغاري” / “nheb na3ref ken fama mouna7 lel sghar.” **One next question when age is unknown:** “قدّاش أعمار صغارك؟” (native-speaker review needed). **Scripted reply:** “One is 4; one is 9.” Route to one `FAMILY_ALLOWANCE_CHILDREN` service with `UNDER_6` and `AGES_6_TO_18` variants; ask about the household's stated Amen registration and whether a child's existing information needs verification/update only after the age answer. These answers are simulated, not checked against a registry. The Ministry's under-6 entry mentions a birth extract for a child not recorded under Amen Social; the 6–18 communiqué does not supply an exhaustive independent dossier. [B1](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/octroi-des-aides-familiales-aux-enfants-qui-sont-moins-de-6-ans), [B2](https://www.social.gov.tn/ar/node/1847), [B3](https://www.social.gov.tn/ar/node/4611)

## Lightweight route C: school social assistance

**Intent:** “Je n'arrive pas à payer les fournitures et le transport scolaire de mon enfant.” **One next question:** “Quel besoin est le plus urgent : fournitures ou transport ?” **Scripted reply:** “Transport.” Route to `SCHOOL_SOCIAL_ASSISTANCE`, show a short need-specific guidance screen and a *synthetic* transport-need photo/readability example. The Ministry service page names school social assistance and needs such as supplies and transport, but describes case-confirming documents generally. A school-attendance note, household situation proof, transport evidence, or medical/disability evidence would require individual confirmation; the demo uses no health/disability fixture. [C1](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/octroi-des-aides-dans-le-cadre-du-programme-d%E2%80%99action-social-en-milieu-scolaire)

| Step / UI state | Input | Mock output and transition | Source provenance | Error handling / UX |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | School cost intent | Offer `SCHOOL_SOCIAL_ASSISTANCE` with visible simulation badge; ask one supplies/transport question. | [C1](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/octroi-des-aides-dans-le-cadre-du-programme-d%E2%80%99action-social-en-milieu-scolaire). | Ambiguous school-aid wording → clarify; do not silently route to the distinct back-to-school measure [C2](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/octroi-des-aides-%C3%A0-la-rentr%C3%A9e-scolaire-et-universitaire). |
| Need and illustration | “Transport”; choose a synthetic unreadable then readable photo | Fields: stated school need, fictional child school-age flag, demo photo readability. Show correction before travel and conditional evidence explanation; stop at preparation/worker-review handoff. | Need examples and case-specific documents: [C1](https://www.social.gov.tn/fr/octroi-des-aides-dans-le-cadre-du-programme-d%E2%80%99action-social-en-milieu-scolaire). Photo/OCR are demo-only. | Unreadable image → request a clearer one. Exact required evidence is `TBD`; do not promise a grant or show a complete official dossier. |

Both B and C must be selectable from later service discovery, but they are intentionally lighter than A. They end with source-linked information, explicit uncertainty, and optional human review; no simulated official approval.

## 30–45 second live pitch cut

1. Enter Fatma's prepared Derja intent; tap the clearly labelled Amen Social simulation route (5–8 s).
2. Show the source-linked, non-exhaustive sample list and select the blurred synthetic birth extract (8–10 s).
3. Point to the low-confidence/missing field; replace it with the clear synthetic image and confirm the correction (10–12 s).
4. Show the demo-only “Prêt pour le rendez-vous de démonstration” status and worker summary (7–10 s). Say: “The correction happened before the trip; the real ULPS still checks the file in person.”

The pitch may skip fake slot selection for time. It must retain the simulation badge and non-exhaustive warning. POC-04 [#10](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/10) can consume the fixed IDs and fixture descriptors; POC-06 [#12](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/12) can consume exact input, clarification, and route branches; POC-08 [#14](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/14) can consume the demo-only state transitions. All remain separate authorized work.
