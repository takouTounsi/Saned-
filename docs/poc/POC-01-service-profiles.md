# POC-01 — service profiles and source register

**Status: DEMO / À VALIDER MAS.** Scope: [#7](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/7), [#35](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/35). This is a product scenario specification for a disposable three-day proof of concept, not an administrative guide. The three programme families below are real Ministry subjects. The people, answers, uploads, checklist, appointment, worker receipt, and application screens are synthetic simulations. No official account, connection, submission, eligibility decision, award, or confirmed ULPS appointment exists in the demo. Real filing and decisions remain with the competent ULPS and human authorities. The four languages planned for the demonstration are French, standard Arabic, Tunisian Derja in Arabic script, and Arabizi; copy needs native-speaker review.

The demonstrator measures only **préparation de dossier / état de complétude du scénario de démonstration**. A green demo checklist covers its selected sample fields, never the full administrative dossier. It must not tell a person that they are eligible, officially validated, officially filed, or that every required administrative document is present.

## Hero user and routes

**Fatma** is the single fictional hero. She is unemployed and has two fictional children, aged 4 and 9 solely to exercise the two child-age routes. She wants to prepare an Amen Social registration file before a visit. Her pain is reaching a counter with a missing or unreadable piece, losing time and needing another trip. The demo should offer short questions, plain explanations, visible progress, readable text, keyboard access, clear error recovery, and language choice without demanding an official identity or account. **Success:** Fatma corrects an unreadable synthetic piece before travel and can show a worker an organized *demo* dossier without mistaking its status for an official decision.

No fixture may contain a valid-looking national identity number (CIN), address, phone, email, medical data, official stamp, or real person's document. Use obvious `DEMO` watermarks and generic placeholders. Child ages are test fixtures, not eligibility findings.

## Data handoff for later tickets, not runtime configuration

The canonical service IDs are `AMEN_SOCIAL_REGISTRATION`, `FAMILY_ALLOWANCE_CHILDREN` (variant IDs `UNDER_6`, `AGES_6_TO_18`), and `SCHOOL_SOCIAL_ASSISTANCE`. `SERVICE_A/B/C` are human-readable aliases, not additional service objects. Proposed display labels are in the route table. Stable intent concepts are `prepare_amen_registration`, `ask_child_family_allowance`, and `ask_school_social_assistance`; these are handoff labels, not a selected API or schema. POC-04 [#10](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/10) owns any later service configuration.

For each later document descriptor, preserve a Ministry URL, its scope, `demo_only`, `needs_review`, and a conditionality note. Set `mandatory` only after MAS/ULPS confirms applicability to the real process; until then it is `TBD`, including A's illustrative subset. A deterministic *demo* checklist may mark a fixture required for the scripted path, with a separate label. POC-06 [#12](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/12) owns scripted language/routing and native review. POC-08 [#14](https://github.com/Medmas07/Inclusa/issues/14) owns demo-only checklist states. No runtime JSON, legal eligibility formula, official dossier-completion rule, integration, or production implementation is specified here.
